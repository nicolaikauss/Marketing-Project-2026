Customer journey

Six steps, one job and one metric each

1 · Aware

Co-signed university email; reps repost in course groups within 48 h; stealth QR cards

% aware (pulse survey)

2 · Consider

“Cosa Naya è / non è” page; privacy in three lines; a Bmind psychologist on camera

Landing page → store

3 · Activate

Group code on the welcome screen, not buried in Settings; starter prompts in Italian

Plus accounts / eligible

4 · First value

“Ciao, sono Naya. Come stai?” → a short first talk that ends with one plan

≥60% send 3+ messages

5 · Return

Check-in two days later: “Com’è andato l’esame?”

≥20% month-2 return

6 · Bridge

In-app route to the university service or a Bmind psychologist

Completed requests, never a target
