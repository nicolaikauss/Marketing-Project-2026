Four things that make Italy different

Privacy is a headline topic

The Garante blocked ChatGPT in 2023 and fined Replika's maker €5M in 2025. Students ask where their chats go before what it does.

AI adoption lags Europe

47% of Italians aged 16–24 use AI tools, against 64% across the EU. Not every student is an AI native.

The exam calendar is the media plan

Winter exams Jan–Feb, summer Jun–Jul, freshers Sep–Oct. Blue Monday falls mid-session; 10 October 2027 is a Sunday.

Universities want a digital front door

UniSiena's ABACUS chatbot books campus psychologists; Udine launched a self-help platform.
